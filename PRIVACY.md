# Privacy Policy — Hermes Agent

_Last updated: 2026-10-11_

## What this app is

**Hermes Agent** is a private, single-user personal automation. It runs on its
owner's own computer. It is not offered to the public, there is no sign-up
flow, and it has exactly one user: the owner of the Google Account that
authorizes it. No other person can grant it access, and it does not operate as
a service on anyone else's behalf.

## What Google user data it accesses

With the owner's explicit consent, through the Google APIs it may:

- read, send and modify **Gmail** messages;
- read and write **Google Drive** files (files it creates, and files the owner
  points it at);
- read and modify **Google Calendar** events;
- read **Google Contacts**;
- read and write **Google Sheets**;
- read and write **Google Docs**.

It accesses only what is needed to carry out the specific task the owner asked
for — uploading or downloading a file, sending an email, looking up a calendar,
updating a document. It does not scan or collect anything beyond that.

## How the data is used, stored and shared

- Data is used **only** to perform the task the owner requested, on the owner's
  own data.
- OAuth tokens and any cached copies are stored **locally on the owner's own
  machine**. This app operates no server, and no copy of the data is sent to
  any third party.
- **No data is sold, rented, or shared with anyone.** It is not used for
  advertising, profiling, or training machine-learning models.
- No person other than the owner ever reads the data.

## Limited Use disclosure

Hermes Agent's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs
adheres to the [Google API Services User Data
Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the **Limited Use** requirements.

## Retention and deletion

The owner may revoke this app's access at any time at
<https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>; revoking it immediately ends all
access. Locally stored tokens and cached copies can be deleted at any time by
the owner by removing them from their own machine.
